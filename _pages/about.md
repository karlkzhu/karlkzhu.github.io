I am a PhD student at the MIT Operations Research Center, advised by Prof. Dimitris Bertsimas. I build optimization and machine learning models, develop new solution methods, and apply them to energy systems, electricity markets, and healthcare. I completed a Bachelor of Engineering with First Class Honours in Engineering Science at the University of Auckland, advised by Prof. Andy Philpott.

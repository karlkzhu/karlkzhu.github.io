I am a PhD student at the MIT Operations Research Center, advised by Prof. Dimitris Bertsimas. My research develops optimization and machine learning models and solution methods, with applications in energy systems, electricity markets, and healthcare. I completed a Bachelor of Engineering with First Class Honours in Engineering Science at the University of Auckland, supervised by Prof. Andy Philpott.
